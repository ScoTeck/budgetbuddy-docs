Budget Buddy — Support

Every answer here describes a screen or behaviour that exists in the app. Tab names, Settings rows and button labels are quoted exactly as they appear.

Getting started

What does Budget Buddy actually do?

It turns your income, bills and savings goal into one number: what you can safely spend today. Log what you spend, and the number updates.

How do I set it up?

Onboarding asks three things: your monthly income, which fixed bills you have, and how much you want to save. It then shows your daily figure. You can change any of it later in Settings → Daily Budget Settings.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no server. Everything lives on your iPhone.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No, and you can't — Budget Buddy has no bank connections. You add transactions yourself, or scan a receipt.

The daily number

How is it calculated?

Income, minus fixed expenses, minus your savings goal, gives what's genuinely spare for the month. The zero-based method (the default) subtracts what you've already spent this month before today, spreads the rest across the days remaining including today, and then subtracts what you've spent today. Spend less today and tomorrow's figure rises; overspend and it falls.

Settings → Daily Budget Settings also offers a Monthly Average method, which simply divides what's spare by the number of days in the current month.

Why did my number change when I didn't spend anything?

Because a day passed. There are fewer days left to spread the same money over, so the daily figure moves. It also updates the moment you change your income, bills or savings goal.

Can it go negative?

No. If your bills and savings goal consume everything you earn, the number is shown as zero rather than a negative figure. The Health tab will tell you the budget doesn't balance.

What is "Daily Dollars"?

The name for that number on the Home screen. Settings → How Daily Dollars Work explains the method in more detail.

Transactions

How do I add one?

Tap the + button in the centre of the tab bar. Enter an amount, optionally a description, category, date and notes, then Save.

How do I see my transactions?

The Insights tab. You can search by description or category name, and filter by category, date range and type (income or expense).

Can I edit or delete a transaction?

Yes — open it from the list in Insights.

Is there a limit on how many I can add?

No, on any plan.

Receipt scanning

How does it work?

In the add-transaction screen, tap Attach Receipt. On an iPhone you can use the document camera; you can also pick an existing photo. Budget Buddy reads the total on-device and shows you what it found, with how confident it is. You confirm or correct the amount before anything is saved.

Is my receipt sent anywhere?

No. Text recognition happens on your device and the image is stored in the app's own storage. Nothing is uploaded.

Is scanning free?

No — receipt scanning is part of Budget Buddy Premium ($4.99/month). Adding transactions by hand is free and unlimited.

The amount it read was wrong

Correct it on the review screen before saving. OCR is a suggestion; the app always asks you to confirm. Accuracy varies with the receipt — faded thermal paper and unusual layouts are harder.

Categories and budgets

How do I add a category?

Settings → Manage Categories → +. Pick a name, icon and colour.

How many can I have?

The free plan includes 5 custom categories, on top of the defaults that ship with the app. Premium removes the limit.

Can I delete a category?

You can delete ones you created, by swiping in Manage Categories. Default categories stay, and a category still in use by a transaction or budget can't be removed.

How do I set a budget for a category?

The Categories tab.

Health, debts and savings

What is the Health tab?

An overall financial health grade from your debts, savings goals and emergency fund, with your net worth and a short list of the most useful things you could do next. From there you can open Debts, Savings Goals and Emergency Fund.

Does it tell me how to pay off debt?

It shows each debt's balance, rate and minimum payment, and estimates a payoff timeline including what extra payments would save. It is not financial advice.

Streaks and achievements

What's the streak?

The number of consecutive days you've opened the app. It shows on Home.

How do I see achievements?

Settings → Achievements. It shows what you've earned, what's in progress with a bar against each target, and what's still to earn.

Do achievements cost anything?

No, they're available to everyone.

Notifications

Can it remind me to log spending?

Yes — Settings → Daily Reminders schedules a reminder at 8pm. iOS will ask permission the first time. If you decline, the switch turns itself back off and tells you why, rather than pretending reminders are on.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage. There is no cloud, no sync, and no server.

Does it sync between devices?

No. Budget Buddy does not sync. Your data stays on the device you entered it on. Use Export All Data (CSV) if you want to move it yourself.

How do I export my data?

Settings → Export All Data (CSV). It produces one CSV containing your settings, categories, budgets, transactions, debts, savings goals and emergency fund, and hands it to the iOS share sheet so you can save or send it.

The file has section headers (lines beginning #) separating each record type, so open it in a spreadsheet rather than expecting a single flat table.

Is export a paid feature?

No. Exporting your own data is free on every plan and always will be.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Clear All Data, then confirm in the dialog. It permanently removes every transaction, budget, category, goal, debt and setting on the device, cancels scheduled reminders, and deletes stored receipt images. It cannot be undone — export first if you want a copy.

Do you collect analytics?

Not currently. The app contains an analytics client, but it is never started, so nothing is transmitted. Settings → Share Anonymous Usage Data records your preference in case that ever changes; leaving it off guarantees nothing is sent. See the Privacy Policy.

Pricing

What does it cost?

Free to use. Premium is $4.99/month and adds two things: receipt scanning, and unlimited custom categories.

Everything else — the daily number, transactions, budgets, categories up to the free limit, debts, savings goals, the emergency fund, the health grade, streaks, achievements, reminders and full data export — is free.

There are no ads on any plan.

How do I subscribe or cancel?

Settings → Upgrade to Premium. Cancel any time in your Apple ID settings; Apple handles billing.

Is there a free trial?

The free plan has no time limit, so there's nothing to trial. Subscribe only if you want scanning or more categories.

Troubleshooting

The app won't open, or my data looks wrong

Force-quit and reopen first. If data still looks wrong, export it before doing anything else, so you have a copy.

I reinstalled and my data is gone

Reinstalling deletes the app's local storage. Because there is no cloud backup, an export or an encrypted iPhone backup is the only way to recover — take an export regularly if your records matter to you.

Reminders aren't arriving

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Budget Buddy. If you previously declined permission, the toggle in the app will be off and will say so.
