Budget Buddy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026 · Applies to Budget Buddy for iPhone, version 1.0

The short version. Budget Buddy keeps your financial data on your iPhone. There is no account, no server of ours, and no bank connection. We do not receive your transactions, balances, receipts, or anything derived from them.

At the time of writing the app transmits nothing at all — not even anonymous usage counts. Section 4 explains the one component that could, why it is currently inactive, and what would have to change before it were.

1. What we collect

Nothing. No personal data reaches us, because there is nowhere for it to go: Budget Buddy has no backend, no user accounts and no authentication.

2. What stays on your device

All of the following is stored locally, in the app's own protected storage, and never leaves your iPhone unless you deliberately export or share it:

  • Transactions: amounts, descriptions, dates, categories, notes
  • Budgets and categories, including ones you create
  • Your income, fixed expenses and savings goal
  • Debts, savings goals and emergency-fund figures
  • Receipt images you capture or select, and the text recognised from them
  • Streaks, achievements and app preferences

iOS protects this storage with device encryption when your iPhone is locked with a passcode.

3. Receipt scanning

Text recognition runs entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Receipt images are saved in the app's storage and are not uploaded anywhere. If you delete the transaction or clear all data, the image is deleted with it.

Camera and photo-library permission are requested only so you can capture or choose a receipt. Neither is required to use the app.

4. Analytics — currently inactive

The app bundles a client for TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany), a privacy-focused analytics service. It is never initialised, so no events are sent. Nothing about your use of the app reaches TelemetryDeck or us today.

Settings → Share Anonymous Usage Data records your preference now, so that if analytics is ever switched on, a choice you already made is honoured rather than assumed. Leaving it off guarantees nothing is ever sent.

If analytics is enabled in a future version, that release must also update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure in the same change, and would send only anonymous feature-usage counts — never amounts, descriptions, category contents or anything identifying you.

5. What we do not do

To be explicit, Budget Buddy does not:

  • Sync to iCloud or anywhere else. There is no cloud copy of your data.
  • Connect to your bank or any financial institution.
  • Collect crash reports.
  • Show advertising, or share data with advertisers.
  • Track you across other apps or websites. The app's privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types.
  • Sell or share your information with anyone. There is nothing to sell.

6. Your data rights

Because your data never leaves your device, you exercise these directly in the app rather than by asking us:

Access and portability — Settings → Export All Data (CSV) produces a single file containing your settings, categories, budgets, transactions, debts, savings goals and emergency fund, and hands it to the iOS share sheet. This is free on every plan and always will be.

Erasure — Settings → Clear All Data, then confirm. This permanently removes every record on the device, cancels scheduled reminders and deletes stored receipt images. Deleting the app removes everything too. Neither is recoverable, so export first if you want a copy.

Rectification — edit or delete any individual record in the app at any time.

Under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA these correspond to access, portability, erasure and rectification. There is no data controller-held copy for us to act on, and no request process to go through.

7. Children

Budget Buddy is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content, but it is designed for people managing their own money. We knowingly collect no data from anyone, including children.

8. Payments

Budget Buddy Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. Purchase and subscription status are checked with Apple's StoreKit on your device. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version collects or transmits anything, this policy will be updated in the same release, not after it, and the change will be described here rather than made silently.
